        Use my location

        Browsers only share your location over a secure (HTTPS) connection, and this device serves over standard HTTP. You can always tap the map to set points instead.

        To turn GPS on in Chrome (Android or desktop), allow this address once:

        1. Open a new tab and go to chrome://flags/#unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure
        2. Enter http://nomad.local in the box
        3. Set it to Enabled and tap Relaunch
        4. Come back and press the ◎ button again

        Safari on iPhone/iPad has no equivalent setting; tap the map to set your start instead. This only affects your own browser.
